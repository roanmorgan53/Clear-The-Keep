# Clear The Keep — Glossary

**Run**: One attempt at the keep, from the first floor until the player dies or reaches the Treasury. Health and gold carry across floors for the whole run. Dying ends the run; restarting begins a new one.

**Keep**: The whole dungeon: a stack of floors the player descends through. The last floor's Ladder Room opens the Belly of the Beast instead of a ladder.

**Floor**: One level of the keep, made of hand-built rooms arranged into a new layout every run, joined by corridors. Deeper floors have more enemies, new enemy types and new obstacles. The player leaves a floor by taking the ladder down, and can never come back up.

**Room**: An enclosed area that owns its own enemies, obstacles and doors. Entering a room that hasn't been cleared locks the player in and releases its first wave. Enemies never leave their room.

**Cleared**: A room is cleared when every enemy from every one of its waves is dead. Clearing a room unlocks its doors.

**Spawn Room**: The room where the player starts a floor. It has no enemies.

**Ladder Room**: The room farthest from the Spawn Room. It has no enemies and holds the ladder down to the next floor. Going down is the player's choice, and once they do, the rest of the floor is left behind. On the last floor it opens the Belly of the Beast instead.
_Avoid_: End Room

**Hurtbox**: The part of a character that can be hit. Every character (player and enemy) has one.

**Hitbox**: The area an attack hits. It is only live during the attack's active frames.

**Wind-up**: The visible lead-in before an enemy attack lands, giving the player a chance to react.

**Invincibility**: A short window after the player is hit (0.5 s by default) when they can't be hit again.

**Obstacle**: Something placed in a room that blocks movement or hurts whoever touches it, such as a pit.

**Gold**: The run's money. Dead enemies drop gold coins that the player picks up. It carries across floors.

**Enemy Type**: A kind of enemy. Every enemy type builds on the same base enemy and can add behaviour of its own.

**Goblin Thief**: A fast enemy type that steals a share of the player's gold and then runs away. Killing it drops everything it stole, and sometimes extra gold of its own.

**Whelp**: A small flying dragon. Its flight makes it harder to hit than a ground enemy.

**Dragon**: The final boss, waiting in the Belly of the Beast. Defeating it opens the Treasury.

**Belly of the Beast**: The boss area where the player fights the Dragon.
_Avoid_: Dragon's Lair, Heart of the Keep, boss room

**Treasury**: The victory room behind the Belly of the Beast, full of the keep's gold. Reaching it wins the run.
_Avoid_: Keep Treasury, Victory Room

**Wave**: A group of enemies a room releases together. Later waves are released after a set time or once enough of the current wave has died.

**Merchant**: A character who sells wares for gold during a run, found in a Merchant Room.

**Merchant Room**: A room on a side branch of a floor, with no enemies, where the Merchant sells healing items and, later, other wares.

**HUD**: The on-screen display of the player's health and gold.
